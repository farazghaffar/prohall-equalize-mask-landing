How to use

Your Equalize routine in four steps

A rinse-out mask that does its work in 5 to 10 minutes.

  1. Wash & towel dry

    Hair should be damp, not dripping.

  2. Apply to lengths & ends

    Skip the roots. A walnut-size scoop is enough for shoulder-length hair.

  3. Wait 5–10 minutes

    Very dry or bleached hair should take the full 10. Longer doesn't help.

  4. Rinse

    Rinse well with cool water, then style as usual.

Safety: external use only. Keep out of reach of children. Avoid eye contact; if contact occurs, rinse with plenty of water. If irritation occurs, stop use and seek medical advice. Store in a cool place away from sunlight and heat.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How often should I use it?

1 to 2 times a week, and always after color or bleach.

Will it wash out my keratin treatment?

No. Smoothing treatments like an acidic environment, so Equalize helps the treatment hold.

Will it protect my color?

Sealed hair holds color in; open hair lets it leak out. Equalize seals the cuticle back down.

Is this a protein treatment?

No, it is a balancing treatment — a different job. Rotate it with your other masks: hydration one wash, protein another, Equalize after any chemical service.

How long does one jar last?

About 2 to 3 months, used 1 to 2 times a week on medium to long hair.
